# Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
- 1 Defining Functions of Several Variables, Computing Partial Derivatives, Directional and Gradient Vectors.
- 2 Evaluation of Iterated Integrals, in Rectangular, Polar, Cylindrical Coordinates and their Applications.
- 3 Line Integral and its Applications, of Work, Circulation and Flux.
- 4 Greens Theorem in Plane, its Tangential and Normal Form, Surface Integrals, Divergence and Stokes theorem, Laplace Transform and its operational Properties.
- 5 Expanding Functions in terms of Fourier Series, Fourier Sin and Cosine Series, Introduction to Fourier Transformation. Sequence and Series.

# Weekly Course Outline
## Week 1
- Functions of several variables, Limit and continuity of Higher dimensions.
## Week 2
- Partial derivatives, Chain Rule, Directional Derivative and Gradient Vectors.
## Week 3
- Double and Iterated Integrals over Rectangles, Double Integrals over General Regions, Area by Double Integration.
## Week 4
- Double integrals in polar form, Triple Integrals in Rectangular Coordinates.
## Week 5
- Triple integral in cylindrical and spherical coordinates, Line Integral
## Week 6
- Vector Fields and line Integrals: Work Circulation and Flux. Path Independence
## Week 7
- Conservative Fields and Potential Functions, Greens Theorem in the Plane, Surfaces and Area.
## Week 8
- Surface Integrals, Stocks Theorem.
## Week 9
- The Divergence Theorem and a Unified theory, Definition of the Laplace Transform.
## Week 10
- Inverse Transforms and Transforms of Derivatives.
## Week 11
- Operational Properties I, Translation on the S-Axis, Translation on the t-Axis, Orthogonal Functions.
## Week 12
- Fourier Series, Fourier Cosine and Sine Series.
## Week 13
- Definition of the Fourier Transform and Example.
## Week 14
- Sequence and Infinite series, Integral Test
## Week 15
- Absolute convergence, ratio and root test
## Week 16
- Not provided in source
