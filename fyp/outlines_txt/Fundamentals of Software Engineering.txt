# Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
- Understand the pros and cons of different SDLC models (C2)
- Select suitable SDLC models for different project situations (C3)
- Discuss key Software Engineering Principles (C2)
- Describe different phases of software development (C2)
- Apply the Divide and Conquer principle to decompose a software project into smaller work items (C3)
- Describe the relationship of Software Engineering to Computer Science and Management Science disciplines (C2)
