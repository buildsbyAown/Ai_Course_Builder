# Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
- Understand data structures and their use
- Select efficient and appropriate data structures for different applications

# Weekly Course Outline
## Week 1
- Time Complexity Analysis and Asymptotic Bounds
## Week 2
- Review of Pointers and class Templates
- Linked Lists: Singly linked lists, doubly linked lists, circular lists and corresponding iterators
- Skip List (optional)
## Week 3
- Stacks (Expression Evaluation), Queues
## Week 4
- Recursion with Time complexity Analysis
## Week 5
- Trees: Binary trees and their traversals, Binary search trees (Insertion, Deletion and Search)
## Week 6
- Height Balanced Binary Search Trees (AVL Trees)
- Priority Queues Or Heaps and heap sort
## Week 7
- Data compression and Huffman coding
## Week 8
- Hashing: Hash tables and hash functions, Collision resolution methods, Universal hashing
- Bit vectors and bloom filters
## Week 9
- Graph: Breadth first search and Depth first search, Finding Paths, Cycles

# Recommended Books

- Adam Drozdek, Data structures and algorithms in C++, Course technology, 2004
- Nell Dale, C++ Plus Data Structures, 3rd Edition, Jones and Bartlett, 2003
- Michael T. Goodrich, Roberto Tamassia and David M. Mount, Data structures and algorithms, 2nd Edition, John Wiley & Sons, 2011
