# Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
- Solve algebraic equations and inequalities by using properties of absolute values.
- Analyze the function and sketching the curve by using properties horizontal/vertical and compressing / stretching.
- Investigation of continuity through limits analytically / graphically.
- Apply the concept of differentiation in real life problem.
- Curve sketching using extrema theory.
- Riemann sum, evaluation of definite & indefinite integral and their applications to compute lengths of curves / area of regions / volume of solids.
- Correctly graphs/formulate the equation of line and plane in R3.
