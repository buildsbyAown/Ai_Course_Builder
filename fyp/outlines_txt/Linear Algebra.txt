# Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
- Use concept of elementary row operations to find the inverse of square matrices, determinant of a matrix and solving the system of linear equations.
- Properties of vectors in 2-space, 3-space and n-space and recognize vector spaces and/or subspaces to compute their bases and its dimension.
- Perform Eigen Value analysis and use it to diagonalize a matrix and/or find its powers.
- Identify inner product spaces and/or perform Gram Schmidt process/QR decomposition using inner products.
- Express a linear transformation graphically and matrices and using their properties to solve problems.
